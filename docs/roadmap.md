This file contains a roadmap for possible future feature implementations.

1. Post Processing (llm) - After finishing the transcription the text gets sent to an LLM endpoint and the LLM corrects the text with better context and understanding
2. Smart formatting (local) - When a text is pasted in the middle of a sentence or right after a word with no space, the spacing, capitilizaiton and formatting should automatically adjust the inserted sentence to fit.
3. Selective formatting - Depending on the app, you can change the formatting. Like on messaging apps, you can have no capitilization or skip punctuation etc.
